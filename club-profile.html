About & goals

Empowering student engineers and creative technologists to build transformative digital solutions, lead collaborative ventures, and pioneer innovation at East Delta University.

We run student-led workshops, hackathons, and industry mentorship across campus. The aim is to use what we learn in class in real development work.

  • Hands-on Engineering: Build production-ready web and AI software prototypes.
  • Mentorship: Train freshers in competitive programming & system architecture.
  • Competition: Represent the university at national hackathons and robotics cups.
